Recently, my team has been working mainly on launching the Linea L2 stack. It has been a long journey, starting with
investigating the prover code with the aim of improving its performance and ending with launching the whole system.

[Here is](https://github.com/distributed-lab/linea-dev-edition) a docker-compose example that can help any team
launch their own L2. It also contains a brief overview of the parts Linea consists of.

For my part, Linea made a good impression on me. Their approach of using their own arithmetization, coupled with their own
proving protocol ([Vortex](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/1633.pdf)) and Gnark, is really novel. I'm proud that my team
became one of the open-source contributors to Linea.
